# narenana brand voice guidelines

Source of truth for all copy on narenana.com, Nanawing, Nanawing 2, Wings and the log viewer. Written from the owner's own words (2026-09-29). Read by the Brand Voice plugin (`brand-voice-enforcement`) and by anyone writing copy for these sites.

## Who we are
narenana is one RC pilot who flies wings and FPV and builds free tools for other pilots. If narenana were a person, it would be the pilot at your flying field who has already tried everything: straight answers, no sales pitch, happy to show you the quickest way to get in the air.

The owner, in his own words:
> RC pilots are often not gamers. They don't have Steam accounts and gaming machines to run AAA graphics. All they need is a real engine to practise the craft. Nanawing aims to take away the hassle: take a pilot from thinking about flying on a sim to actually flying in two minutes. Every feature removes what isn't essential. No install. No login. Offline browser cache. Mobile support coming. The goal is to let you fly anywhere and still keep the physics real.

## Audience
- RC pilots in India first, most of them **not gamers**: everyday laptops, no Steam, little patience for setup.
- New wing pilots who want to practise launches, turns and landings before risking a real plane.
- Experienced wing and FPV pilots who want real physics and a fair race, and who spot hype instantly.
- Buyers comparing RC plane prices and stock across Indian sellers.

## Messaging pillars (in priority order)
1. **Real flying, no hassle.** Free, in the browser, no install, no login, flying in about two minutes on the computer you have. Works offline after the first visit.
2. **Built for the craft, not for gamers.** Real flight physics over flashy graphics. Your own RC radio, iNAV-style OSD, launches, circuits and landings.
3. **Made by a pilot.** The owner flies and films his own flights. Proof comes from pilots, e.g. Giz FPV: "Whoever made this… they are an actual FPV pilot."
4. **Honest buying help (Wings).** Current prices and stock across Indian sellers, how we check them, and a link straight to the seller. We don't sell anything.

## Voice attributes (constant everywhere)

**Plain-spoken**
- We are: short, direct sentences; everyday words; say the thing.
- We are not: slogan-speak, taglines, wordplay, or "brand voice" theatre.
- Sounds like: "Open it in your browser and fly. No download, no account."
- Doesn't sound like: "Your first flight. Your fastest lap."

**Specific**
- We are: concrete nouns and verbs from real flying: hand launch, circuit, flare, rates, USB radio, Windmill Run, ₹ prices, 12 sellers.
- We are not: abstract promises such as "experience", "journey" or "find your flow".
- Sounds like: "Practise hand launches and landings until the timing is automatic."
- Doesn't sound like: "Learn the feel of a wing."

**Pilot-to-pilot**
- We are: peer-level. We assume the reader flies or wants to, respect the craft, and are a little dry.
- We are not: hype, gamer bravado, or talking down to beginners.
- Sounds like: "Plug in your radio and it flies like your real setup."
- Doesn't sound like: "Great pilots. Even greater rivals."

**Honest**
- We are: we only claim what the product does today; features that aren't shipped are labelled "coming"; prices are "last checked".
- We are not: superlatives without proof ("best", "ultra-realistic"), or promises we can't keep.
- Sounds like: "Mobile support is coming. For now, fly on any laptop or desktop."
- Doesn't sound like: "Fly anywhere, on any device."

## Tone by context
| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Homepage hero, CTAs | confident, fast, outcome first ("Fly Nanawing now: free, in your browser") |
| Beginner guidance | patient, encouraging, practical steps |
| Racing / leaderboard | competitive but understated; name the course and the time, skip the trash talk |
| Wings (buying) | factual, careful, transparent about how prices are checked |
| About / contact | first person, warm, brief |
| Errors / 404 | plain, helpful, no jokes |

## Banned patterns (AI tells)
- Two-beat slogan pairs: "X. Y." taglines, "Built by a pilot. Made for pilots.", "Good flights. Better stories."
- Rhetorical-question headlines: "How fast?", "The next name up here?"
- Personification and cute conceits: "Give the podium something to worry about", "Every flight tells a story", "A little closer to flying".
- Vague aspiration words: explore, discover, unlock, elevate, journey, experience, seamless, immersive, next-level, "your next favourite".
- Rhythm triads and em-dash drama ("Clean lines. Full commitment.").
- Stating the obvious ("The full review, right here.").

## Style rules
- Indian/British spelling: practise (verb), practice (noun), favourite, colour, manoeuvre.
- Sentence case for headings. Uppercase only for small eyebrow labels.
- Numbers: numerals for specs and counts (2 minutes, 12 sellers, 1700 mm); ₹ with Indian digit grouping (₹29,975).
- CTAs: verb-first and say what happens ("Fly Nanawing", "Compare prices", "Open the log viewer"). Never "Learn more" or "Click here".
- Product names: Nanawing, Nanawing 2, Wings, Log viewer. Course and aircraft names exactly as in the sim.

## Terminology
| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| RC pilot, pilot | gamer, player |
| flight simulator, sim | game |
| practise (verb) | train, grind |
| in your browser, no install | cloud, web app |
| RC radio / transmitter | controller (except gamepad) |
| prices last checked | live prices (unless truly real-time) |
