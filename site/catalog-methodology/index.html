Prices and stock, with the checks explained.

Wings compares RC aircraft listings from Indian sellers. You buy from the seller; narenana does not take payment or fulfil the order.

What gets listed

Only approved listings linked to a published aircraft model appear in the catalog. We group listings by aircraft and show configuration, pack quantity and condition where available. Approval is a catalog review, not a guarantee of a seller’s service, warranty or delivery.

How prices and stock are refreshed

The pipeline scans active seller sources in daily sweeps and checks approved product pages, prioritising older checks. Jobs run in small scheduled batches. A full refresh can take longer when a seller blocks access, a queue is busy or checks are paused.

Each seller row shows the date its data was last checked or seen. These are observations from a seller’s page, not a promise of checkout availability. Open the seller’s listing to confirm the final price, stock, shipping and taxes.

When information is uncertain

Large price changes and product-identity mismatches are flagged for review. A listing under review keeps its stock status, but its price is withheld and shown as “Price under review” until it is checked. Missing prices stay blank, and unavailable or removed offers are labelled separately. Product pages can remain useful when every seller is temporarily out of stock.

Compare like with like

A kit, PNP aircraft, ready-to-fly package and multi-pack can include very different equipment. Check the configuration and condition before comparing totals. Used listings are labelled separately when the source identifies them.

Spotted a mismatch?

Contact narenana with the aircraft and seller links so the listing can be checked.
