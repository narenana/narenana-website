Compare sellers’ prices for the same RC plane

Wings puts Indian sellers’ listings for a plane on one page, and every row shows when its price and stock were last checked. You buy straight from the seller.

Pick the right listing at a glance

Each row shows the seller, the configuration, the price and whether it’s in stock, with a pre-owned tag when the seller says the plane is used. Each listing is reviewed before it goes up.

Spot a stale or wrong price before you buy

Each row says when it was last checked. Open the seller’s listing to confirm the final price, stock, shipping and taxes before you pay.

A price we’re not sure about shows “Price under review” instead of a number until it’s checked again. Out-of-stock and gone listings are marked, and a plane’s page stays up when every seller has run out, so you can still see who sells it.

Compare kits and ready-to-fly planes like for like

A kit, a PNP plane, a ready-to-fly package and a multi-pack come with very different gear. Check the configuration and condition before you compare totals.

Tell us about a wrong listing

Send us the seller’s link and tell us which plane it really is, and we’ll check it.
