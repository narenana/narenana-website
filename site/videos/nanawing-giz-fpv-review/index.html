A wing pilot’s perspective

Giz FPV takes Nanawing for a flight and discusses how it feels from a pilot’s perspective. Watch the original review above, then try the simulator yourself. The review belongs to Giz FPV and is embedded from their YouTube channel.

Try what you see

Nanawing runs in your browser with keyboard, gamepad or a compatible RC radio. New pilots can practise launches, turns and landings; experienced pilots can fly the gate courses and compare their best laps.
