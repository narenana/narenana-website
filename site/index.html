Nanawing / Learn to fly a wing

Do your crashing here first

Practise launches, banked turns and landings at your desk, then take them to the field. Reset and go again, nothing to repair.

  1. Plug in your own RC radio

    Put your radio in USB joystick mode and it flies like your real setup. A gamepad, a keyboard or two thumb sticks on your phone work too.

  2. Launch it and climb out

    A helper throws your wing over their head or on a run-up, depending on the wing, and you fly the climb-out.

  3. Start in chase view, then go FPV

    Watch the wing’s attitude from behind first. Switch to FPV when you’re ready, then try Real FPV, a 140° goggle-style view.

  4. Watch your LiPo on the OSD

    Keep an eye on the pack the way you would in goggles, and bring it home before it runs flat.

Nanawing / Pick your wing

Start on a forgiving trainer, work up to 323 km/h

Learn on the Nanawing One, then take the TBS Chupito, Darkwing or Sukhoi when your landings feel easy.

  • Start here

    Nanawing One

    It keeps flying down to 24 km/h, which gives you time to practise launches, circuits and landings.

    191 km/h241°/s roll0.95 m↗
  • The step up

    Morok UAV

    It rides the gusts and holds a steady line. Get used to flying fast here before the Sukhoi.

    249 km/h201°/s roll0.85 m↗
  • Work up to it

    Sukhoi S-70 Okhotnik

    Heavy and fast. It tops out at 323 km/h, so start setting up your turns sooner.

    323 km/h166°/s roll1.05 m↗
  • For tight gaps

    WUDFLY Spectre

    A light 215 g twin to weave between Parkland’s trees.

    100 km/h344°/s roll0.60 m↗
  • For quick turns

    TBS Chupito

    Snaps round at 360°/s on 6S. Practise on it before you throw the real one.

    140 km/h360°/s roll0.80 m↗
  • For fast passes

    Darkwing

    Open the throttle on 6S and it builds speed in a hurry, up to 270 km/h. Line it up for long, fast passes.

    270 km/h240°/s roll1.00 m↗
