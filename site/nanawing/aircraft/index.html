01 / GET CLOSER TO THE SCENERY

WUDFLY Spectre

Aircraft design by WUDFLY.

Small wing. Quick turns. A new reason to take the scenic route. Our recreation of WUDFLY's 600 mm, 215 g twin-motor wing pairs the lowest top speed in the hangar with the quickest roll. Its measured 100 km/h top speed gives you more time to read the scenery; its crisp bank response invites you to get closer.

Bring a controller and discover its party trick: differential thrust. The yaw stick varies the two motors' speeds to swing the nose, giving the Spectre a move no other wing in the hangar has. It works best at lower speeds and needs more than 10% throttle. A keyboard still handles pitch, roll and throttle; yaw needs a controller.

Our simulator model is an original recreation of WUDFLY's design, built from public product photos. Its handling is a simulator approximation, not a manufacturer-validated flight model.

04 / CHASE THE RUSH

Sukhoi S-70 Okhotnik

Model: “Sukhoi S-70 Okhotnik” by Muhamad Mirza Arrafi, CC BY 4.0. Rendered in Nanawing with its simulator materials.

For the moment you want everything to arrive faster. At a measured 323 km/h in Nanawing, the Sukhoi is the fastest wing in the hangar, with the strongest climb to match. Open it up and watch the scenery rush past.

That speed asks something of you. The highest stall speed and slowest roll of the four mean you'll need to plan your banks and commit to your line. Find the rhythm, and every clean gate feels earned.

Where the numbers come from

A test bot flies each aircraft on the same flight model you play with. A settled full-throttle level run gives top speed, a full-stick roll gives roll rate, and a 20° full-power climb gives climb rate.

Stall speed is the unpowered, wings-level 1 g lift limit from each wing's aerodynamic curve. The in-game stall warning comes earlier, at 27, 32, 39 and 31 km/h for the One, Morok, Sukhoi and Spectre, and banked turns need more speed. The bench is rerun after any tuning, so these numbers match what you fly.

Questions

Which Nanawing aircraft should I fly first?

Try the Spectre for a slower pace and quick banks close to the scenery. Brand new to flying? The Nanawing One is a welcoming starting point, with the lowest stall speed in the hangar. Choose the Morok for more speed, or the Sukhoi for the fastest flight of the four.

Is the WUDFLY Spectre in Nanawing the real aircraft?

It's our recreation of WUDFLY's design, with the real wing's 600 mm span, twin motors and white-and-yellow livery. Its handling is a simulator approximation. The real Spectre is designed and sold by WUDFLY at wudfly.com.

Can every aircraft race every course?

Yes. Each of the four courses keeps a separate board for each aircraft, so you race pilots in the same plane. Only your best lap counts.

Do I need a controller?

No. All four fly on a keyboard, and a gamepad or a USB RC radio works too. The Spectre's yaw needs a controller's yaw stick.
