Learn a new wing without a repair bill

Learning a new wing is the fun part: how much stick it wants, when to ease off the throttle, how early to start a turn. The forgiving trainer, the nimble twin-motor WUDFLY Spectre, the quick-rolling TBS Chupito, the hard-accelerating Darkwing and the fast, heavy Sukhoi each give you something new to work on.

Try them all. Repeat a manoeuvre until it's clean, fly a new line or race a course, and when you crash, reset and go again. Plug in a gamepad or USB RC radio to practise on sticks, or start on the keyboard or your phone.

01 / MADE FOR TIGHT GAPS

WUDFLY Spectre

Aircraft design by WUDFLY.

Weave the WUDFLY Spectre through tight gaps with small, deliberate stick inputs. It tops out at 100 km/h, slower than any other wing in the hangar, so you have time to line up each one.

On the Spectre you get yaw too: push the left stick sideways on a gamepad or your phone, or use the yaw stick on your RC radio, and the nose swings. Try it at a gentle pace first. Yaw comes in once the throttle is past 10%.

Then take it to Parkland and race Estate Run. Thread the green start arch, then 14 gates in 2.8 km: through a colonnade, a barn and the forks of giant trees, and under the estate bridge.

05 / QUICKEST ROLL IN THE HANGAR

TBS Chupito

Reference airframe by Team BlackSheep.

Fly Team BlackSheep's 800mm pusher wing, recreated in Nanawing. It snaps round at 360°/s, the quickest roll in the hangar, so you can flick it from bank to bank between gates. Then open it up to 140 km/h on its 6S 1800 mAh pack.

It stalls at 35 km/h, so keep your speed up through the turns, then ease it down gently when you land.

Race it on all 5 courses, from Windmill Run on the Island to Estate Run through Parkland's giant oaks.

06 / BUILT FOR FAST PASSES

Darkwing

Aircraft design by Anirudh.

Fly Anirudh's 3D-printed twin-motor pusher wing, recreated in Nanawing. It gets to 270 km/h in a hurry on its 6S 5000 mAh pack, so build speed on the straights and carry it through wide, smooth turns.

Near full throttle it twists left, as the real one does: hold a little right roll and it tracks straight. It stalls at 39 km/h, the highest in the hangar, so keep your speed up through the turns and bring it in gently to land.

Race it on Windmill Run and River Run on the Island, and on Tunnel Run and Pyramid Run in the Desert.

Questions

How does this help me at the RC field?

Practise stick inputs, throttle management and planning your turns, and feel how different aircraft respond.

Which Nanawing aircraft should I fly first?

Start with the Nanawing One if you are learning the controls. Try the WUDFLY Spectre for tight gaps and yaw. Move to the Morok and Sukhoi to feel how more speed changes your timing, to the TBS Chupito for the quickest roll in the hangar, and to the Darkwing for speed that builds in a hurry. Every aircraft is free, so you can try them all.

Are the WUDFLY Spectre, the TBS Chupito and the Darkwing in Nanawing the real aircraft?

No. They're our recreations of WUDFLY's, Team BlackSheep's and Anirudh's wings. The WUDFLY Spectre keeps the real wing's 600mm span, twin motors and white and yellow colours; the TBS Chupito keeps its 800mm span and single pusher prop; the Darkwing keeps its glossy black 3D-printed airframe and twin counter-rotating props. Their handling is a simulator approximation, and the TBS Chupito's is not TBS flight data; the Darkwing's is tuned from flights of the real aircraft. WUDFLY designs and sells the real Spectre at wudfly.com, Team BlackSheep sells the real Chupito at team-blacksheep.com, and Anirudh designed the real Darkwing (instagram.com/anirud.aero).

Which aircraft race which course?

All 6 aircraft race Windmill Run and River Run on the Island, and Tunnel Run and Pyramid Run in the Desert. Take the WUDFLY Spectre or the TBS Chupito to Parkland for Estate Run. Race the pilots flying your wing: set a time, see where you rank, then go back and beat it.

Do I need a gamepad or RC radio?

No. All 6 fly on a keyboard or on your phone's thumb sticks. A gamepad or USB RC radio works too: plug it in and set it up in the Hangar's Controls tab. To yaw the WUDFLY Spectre, use a gamepad, an RC radio or your phone's left stick.

Sim time is no substitute for instruction, aircraft checks or safe flying at the field.
