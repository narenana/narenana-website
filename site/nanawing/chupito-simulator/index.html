3 things to practise

Snap it round

Flick it from bank to bank between the gates with small stick inputs, so the real one doesn’t surprise you. It rolls at 360°/s, the quickest roll in the hangar.

Keep your speed up

Open it up to 140 km/h on the straights. It stalls at 35 km/h, so carry speed through the turns and ease it down gently when you land.

Race it on all 5 courses

Fly Windmill Run and River Run on the Island, Tunnel Run and Pyramid Run in the Desert, then thread Estate Run’s giant oaks over the estate roofs, a 2.8 km run. Take the Chupito or the WUDFLY Spectre. Set a lap time, see where you rank, then go back and beat it.

Fly it with what you have

Keyboard, gamepad or your own USB RC radio on a laptop; two thumb sticks on your phone. No gaming PC needed: most laptops from the last few years are plenty.

Without a radio you can still fly it clean on the keyboard: tap for a nudge, hold for full stick. Press Space for a two-handed throw over your head and it’s away. Keep an eye on your battery on the iNAV-style OSD.

What will bite you

  • It stalls at 35 km/h. A slow, tight turn is how a Chupito stalls. Keep the speed up and start the turn early.
  • The roll is quick. Small inputs, then wait; the wing does the rest.
  • Landing: ease it down and keep a little throttle in. Chop the throttle early and it drops.
  • Crash in the sim, then reset and go again. Repeat a manoeuvre until it’s clean, then take it to the field.

Print the parts other pilots already designed

Each link goes to the designer’s own page on MakerWorld or Thingiverse, with the notes and downloads there. Test-fit each print on your own build first.

Keep the camera and VTX where you put them

  • TBS Chupito 800 Pan-Tower ↗ninjamic85 · MakerWorld

    Pan the camera on a Feetech FT90M servo and look into the turn. It is the mount Team BlackSheep links from the kit page, and the designer links a DJI O4 Pro mount for it.

  • TBS Chupito camera pod with a lock ↗DJISilesia · Thingiverse

    A camera pod that locks between the battery-tray ribs, so a hard throw can’t push the VTX assembly back. Lift the lock with a finger to take it out.

  • TBS Chupito VTX Cage lock ↗JP75 · MakerWorld

    A 2 g TPU block that stops the stock VTX support sliding back when you throw.

  • TBS Chupito O4 Pro 10 degree soft mount ↗BobbyFPV · MakerWorld

    Soft-mounts a DJI O4 Pro on M2 gummies, tilts it 10° down and leaves room for ND filters.

A hatch and a battery that stay put

  • TBS Chupito Battery Holder ↗FPV Tribe · MakerWorld

    Lets shorter 5-inch quad packs sit tight under the stock strap. One version takes an Ummagrip plate, so the CG stays where you set it from pack to pack.

  • TBS Chupito Battery Tray 2 Stap ↗GibroniFPV · Thingiverse

    A TPU tray with two straps in the middle, for when the single stock strap won’t hold a balanced pack.

  • Screw Down Hatch for Team BlackSheep Chupito ↗GibroniFPV · Thingiverse

    If the foam hatch is gone, this one screws down with an M3 instead of clipping. Stretch it 2mm before printing; the designer’s page says why.

Protect the fins, servos and wings

  • TBS Chupito Bottom Fin Slider ↗FPV Tribe · MakerWorld

    A skid for the bottom fin, so landings on tarmac wear the print and not the foam. Tape or hot-glue it on.

  • TBS Chupito Magnetic Wing Connectors ↗BMS Racing · MakerWorld

    Slide the wings on with a magnetic click, no servo leads to plug in. Uses the spring-loaded 4-pin connectors the designer links.

  • Servo air cover TBS Chupito ↗voba18 · MakerWorld

    Hides the servo arms for a cleaner wing. Print it in ASA or nylon and glue it on.

Keep the electronics bay tidy

  • TBS Chupito FPV Wing USB-C Board Mount ↗BMS Racing · MakerWorld

    Tape a USB-C board to the print and clip it into the bay, so the port stays reachable.

  • Chupito FC Deck ↗cooties · MakerWorld

    Replaces the plywood deck: 20×20mm or 30.5mm mounts for the flight controller, a slot to zip-tie the ESC underneath and a spot for the GPS at the back.

Hang it up between flights

  • Wall Mount Hanger for TBS Chupito FPV Wing ↗TacGriz · MakerWorld

    Hang the wing on the wall by its motor with one screw; made for the standard TBS electronics package.

Electronics and shells differ, so the fit may vary.

Questions

Is this the real TBS Chupito?

It’s Nanawing’s recreation of Team BlackSheep’s 800mm wing, with the same span and shape. It is not an official TBS simulator. Practise the habits here; the kit itself comes from Team BlackSheep.

Do I need an RC radio?

No. Fly on the keyboard, a gamepad or your own USB RC radio. On the keyboard, tap for a nudge and hold for full stick.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Open the same link on your phone and fly with two thumb sticks on the screen.

Is it free, and do I have to install anything?

It’s free and runs in your browser: no install, no login. After the first visit it works offline too.

Where can I buy a TBS Chupito in India?

Compare Chupito prices and stock at Indian hobby sellers on Wings, then buy from each seller’s page. Compare TBS Chupito prices in India.

Are there 3D-printed parts for the Chupito?

Yes. Pilots have shared prints for the camera, battery, hatch and fins, free on MakerWorld and Thingiverse. See the prints.

Can I watch my real Chupito flight back afterwards?

Yes. Drop in the iNAV or Betaflight blackbox log, or the EdgeTX telemetry CSV, and watch it on a 3D globe with the charts alongside. Free in your browser; the log stays on your machine. Open the log viewer.
