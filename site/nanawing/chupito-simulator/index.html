What you’ll be doing

Snap it round

It rolls at 360°/s, the quickest roll in the hangar. Flick it from bank to bank between the gates and get a feel for how little stick it wants, so the real one doesn’t surprise you.

Keep your speed up

Open it up to 140 km/h on the straights. It stalls at 35 km/h and warns you at 44, so carry speed through the turns and ease it down gently when you land.

Race it on all 5 courses

Windmill Run and River Run on the Island, Tunnel Run and Pyramid Run in the Desert, and Estate Run: 2.8 km through Parkland’s giant oaks and over the estate roofs, raced in the Chupito or the WUDFLY Spectre. Set a lap time, see where you rank, then go back and beat it.

Fly it with what you have

Keyboard, gamepad or your own USB RC radio on a laptop; two thumb sticks on your phone. No gaming PC: most laptops from the last few years run it.

On the keyboard a tap nudges the wing and a hold gives you full stick, so you can fly it clean without a radio. Press Space for a two-handed throw over your head and it’s away. Keep an eye on your battery on the iNAV-style OSD.

What will bite you

  • It stalls at 35 km/h. A slow, tight turn is how a Chupito meets the ground. Keep the speed up and start the turn early.
  • The roll is quick. Small inputs, then wait; the wing does the rest.
  • Landing: ease it down and keep a little throttle in. Chop it early and see the first point.
  • Crash in the sim, reset, go again. Repeat a manoeuvre until it’s clean, then take it to the field.

Questions

Is this the real TBS Chupito?

It’s Nanawing’s recreation of Team BlackSheep’s 800 mm wing: the same span and shape, with the sim’s own handling, which is not TBS flight data. Nanawing is not an official TBS simulator. Practise the habits here; the kit itself comes from Team BlackSheep.

Do I need a controller?

No. Fly on the keyboard, a gamepad or your own USB RC radio. The keyboard is tuned for the Chupito: a tap nudges, a hold gives full stick.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Open the same link on your phone and fly with two thumb sticks on the screen.

Is it free, and do I have to install anything?

It’s free and runs in your browser: no install, no login. After the first visit it works offline too.
