Nanawing 2 / Your practice field

Get used to the plane flying towards you

Bring it back towards yourself on every circuit until you stop banking the wrong way.

  1. Read the wind off the windsock

    Land into the wind it shows you, and set up the approach the way you would at the field.

  2. Keep the model in view

    Turn on tracking and zoom when it gets small, then fly without them once your eyes can keep up.

  3. Fly from the pilot’s spot

    See the strip and the sky the way you would with your radio in hand.

Nanawing 2 / Flight practice

Practise the whole flight, launch to landing

Throw it or roll it down the runway, then bring it back along the strip. After every landing you’ll know if it was clean, rough or a crash.

Nanawing 2 / Questions pilots ask

Get set up before your first circuit

Is it really free?

Yes. No install, no login, no ads and no paid aircraft. It’s a personal project by narenana, an RC pilot.

Do I need an RC radio or a gamepad?

No. All four flight controls work on a keyboard. A gamepad or an RC radio gives you sticks to practise with; set them up under Controls in the Hangar.

Can I use my RadioMaster or EdgeTX radio?

Yes, in USB joystick mode. Plug it in and follow the calibration wizard: it maps the four flight controls, with optional trim buttons. If the wizard can’t see your radio, check its USB mode or try another browser.

Can I fly offline?

Yes, once it has loaded fully online. Settings in the sim shows when it’s ready to fly offline. Keep the site data for nanawing2.narenana.com and it stays ready.

Will it work on my phone?

To fly Nanawing 2, use a computer or a large tablet with a keyboard, gamepad or USB RC radio. On your phone, tap “Send it to your computer” and open the link there. Nanawing, the FPV sim, flies on a phone with two thumb sticks.

Does it fly exactly like my real model?

Not exactly: your own plane will handle differently. Use it to practise orientation, control and approaches until they come naturally at the field.

What’s the difference between Nanawing and Nanawing 2?

Nanawing 2 is line of sight: you stand beside the runway and watch the model. Nanawing puts you on board an FPV wing and lets you race other pilots. Both are free, with no install or login.
